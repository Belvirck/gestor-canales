Privacy Policy - Gestor de Canales

Last updated: 23 November 2026

  1. Who we are. Gestor de Canales is a personal tool run by a single individual for his own use. Contact: tonibeltravicedo@gmail.com
  2. What data is used. When the owner connects his TikTok account, the app receives basic profile information (user.info.basic) and an access token. The app also handles the videos, titles and hashtags that the owner chooses to upload.
  3. Where data is stored. All data and tokens are stored only in the owner's own browser on his own computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.
  4. Sharing. Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. It is only sent to TikTok to upload the owner's own videos.
  5. Other users. The app does not collect or process any data from other TikTok users.
  6. Retention and deletion. The owner can disconnect the account and delete all stored data at any time by clearing the browser data or revoking access in TikTok's settings.
  7. Changes. This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
  8. Contact. tonibeltravicedo@gmail.com
